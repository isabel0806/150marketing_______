# Global Voice Rules (all LinkedIn skills)

## Hard rules

1. **No em dashes** (`—`), en dashes (`–`), or double dashes (`--`). Biggest AI tell. Use `..` as a soft pause instead when you'd reach for one.
2. **Capitalize personal names, company names, product names.** Lowercase reads as disrespectful.
3. **Sentence starts can be lowercase** (natural voice), but names inside are always capitalized.
4. **Don't mention the user's own product by name** in comments/replies on third-party posts. Describe what they do instead.
5. **Never paste generic praise** ("Great post!", "This.", "100%", "Couldn't agree more").

## Vocabulary blacklist

Never use:
- leverage, utilize, facilitate, streamline, robust, seamless, delve, navigate, unlock, harness, foster, cultivate
- fundamentally, essentially, ultimately, crucially, notably
- landscape, ecosystem, paradigm, realm, tapestry, journey
- "It's not just X, it's Y"
- "In today's fast-paced world"
- "Game-changer", "deep dive", "at the end of the day", "move the needle"

## Structure

- Vary sentence length aggressively — mix 3-word and 25-word sentences.
- One concrete number or named entity per ~100 words, minimum.
- One line that could be screenshotted and quoted standalone.
- Never end with "What do you think?" — dead prompt. End with a specific question or a clean landing.

## Anti-patterns (always refuse/strip)

- All-caps openers
- Thesis restatement ("so true, AI is changing everything")
- Rule of three without receipts ("faster, cheaper, better")
- Passive voice over ~10% of clauses
- External links in the body (move to first comment)
- Recycled engagement-bait closers ("tag someone who needs this")

## Algorithmic comment scoring (NLP-level, LinkedIn's ranker)

- **Depth** — comments with ≥12 words and multiple sentence structures score higher
- **New keywords** — introduce at least one noun/concept NOT already in the parent post
- **Questions** — end with one that invites a sub-thread
- **Sub-thread sparks** — comments that generate replies from the author AND other commenters are a strong signal

Before submitting a comment, check: does it add at least one noun/concept not already in the post? If no, rewrite.
